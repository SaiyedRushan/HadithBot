# 80% of Quranic Words: what the word match would use

From MugUp deck 103 ("80% of Quranic Words"). Each theme below would be one day's
match: everyone gets the same theme that day, and the game shows the Arabic word with
its transliteration on one card and the English meaning on the other.

**24 themes kept, 286 words.** At one theme a day, that's about 3 and a half weeks before a theme repeats.
The 12 grammar themes are listed at the end and would be left out.

Rows marked ⚠️ have a grammar note in the meaning (like "(pl.)" or "(feminine)"). Say if you want those dropped or reworded.

## Left out: the 12 grammar themes

These pair a word with a grammatical form or rule, not with a plain meaning, so they're not in the game.

- **Commands, Prohibitions & Conditional Sentences** (12), for example: اصْبِرُوا (isbiru) → be patient (pl.)
- **Conjugation: Reading Person, Tense & Voice** (12), for example: كُنتُمْ (kuntum) → you (m. pl.) were
- **Conjunctions, Conditions & Sequence** (11), for example: إِذَا (idha: with a fa answer) → whenever … then
- **Core Prepositions & Their مَا Compounds** (12), for example: بِمَا (bima) → because of what
- **Negation & Exception** (10), for example: لَيْسَتْ (laysat) → she / it is not
- **Particles of Emphasis, Future & Purpose** (10), for example: لَٰكِنْ (lakin) → but (sukun form)
- **Pronouns: Detached & Attached** (12), for example: ـهُ (-hu) → his / him
- **Quantity, Comparison & Possession** (12), for example: وَاحِدَة (wahidah) → one (feminine)
- **Question Words & Demonstratives** (11), for example: هَٰذِهِ (hadhihi) → this (f.)
- **Singulars & Broken Plurals** (12), for example: قَلْب (qalb) → qulub
- **Verb Forms II-X: How the Pattern Changes the Meaning** (12), for example: بَشَّرَ (bashshara) → II: fa''ala
- **Verbs That Change Meaning with Their Preposition** (12), for example: آمَنَ بِ (amana bi) → believed in
