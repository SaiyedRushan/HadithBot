Verify any hadith that matters to you against a scholarly source. Don't want it any more? Run /bismillah stop and remove it.

2. What HadithBot is

HadithBot posts a daily hadith and the 99 names of Allah to channels you choose, and answers on-demand slash commands. It is free, with no paid tier, no accounts, and no advertising. The source code is public at github.com/SaiyedRushan/HadithBot.

It's maintained by one person in their spare time, and these terms are written to be honest about what that means.

3. Who can add and configure it

Discord requires the Manage Server permission to add any bot to a server. Within your server, the /bismillah setup, stop, and status commands require the Manage Channels permission. You're responsible for who you grant those permissions to.

6. Religious content

Hadith texts, translations, and the names of Allah are drawn from publicly available collections, and each hadith names its book and chapter with a link to Sunnah.com so you can check it at the source.

Translations and compiled datasets can contain errors, and grading and interpretation are matters for qualified scholars. HadithBot is a reminder, not a religious authority, and nothing it posts should be treated as a ruling or as a substitute for scholarly guidance. If you spot an error in the text, please report it on GitHub so it can be corrected.
