### 📢 HadithBot update

📗 **A verse of the Quran every day.** From today, each day's message starts with a verse of the Quran in Arabic and English. Under it are three buttons:
- **Read tafsir** opens Ibn Kathir's explanation of the verse on Quran.com
- **Listen** opens the verse on Quran.com, where you can play the recitation
- **Ask ChatGPT** opens ChatGPT with a question about the verse already typed in. ChatGPT isn't a scholar, so check anything important with someone knowledgeable.

**Don't want the verse in this channel?** Anyone with the Manage Channels permission can turn it off by running this here:
`/bismillah setup quran_verse:False`
Only the verse stops. The hadith and the name of Allah keep coming, and you keep your place in the book. To bring the verse back, run it again with `quran_verse:True`.
