### 📢 HadithBot update

- 🎮 **A daily name match game.** The last message of each day now has a Play button. It opens a short game right here in Discord: match 10 names of Allah to their meanings, including today's name. Everyone in the server gets the same 10 and one try, and that message shows everyone's scores. It works in the Discord app on your phone or computer, but not yet in a web browser (we're fixing that). An admin can turn it off with `/bismillah setup name_match:False`.
- 💬 **New Ask ChatGPT button.** It opens ChatGPT with the hadith already typed in, so you can ask what it means. ChatGPT isn't a scholar, so check anything important with someone knowledgeable.
- 📖 **At most 3 hadiths and 3 names a day.** Some servers picked a high number and it became too much to keep up with, so each channel now gets at most 3 of each. New channels start at 1 and 1, and if yours was set higher, it now sends 3.

**Why we recommend 1 a day.** The Prophet ﷺ said: "The most beloved deed to Allah is the most regular and constant even if it were little." (Sahih al-Bukhari). One hadith you read every day is worth more than ten you scroll past.

To switch, an admin can run `/bismillah setup hadiths_per_day:1 names_per_day:1` in this channel. You keep your place in the book.

Want more than 3 a day? Email support@hadithbot.app with your server's name and we'll raise the limit for your channel.
